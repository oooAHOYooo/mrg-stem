🧅 Onion Skin

Onion skinning shows a faint ghost of your previous drawing underneath so you can see how much to move your character!

  1. Draw frame 1
  2. Add a new frame (+)
  3. Turn ON Onion skin (🧅 in toolbar)
  4. Draw frame 2 slightly moved!
