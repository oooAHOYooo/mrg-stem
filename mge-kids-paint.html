✨ Pro Tips for Kids!

🖼️ Tracing Mode

Click on one of the tiny Starter pictures at the top to load a fun trace image! Press 🔀 Shuffle to see more.

Want to trace your own favorite picture?

  1. Find a picture you like on Google.
  2. Right-click the picture and pick "Copy Image Address".
  3. Right-click inside the "URL..." box at the top and pick "Paste".
  4. Click Load!

🧅 What is Onion Skin?

Onion Skinning is a cool trick used by animators to create smooth movement!

When you turn it ON, you will see a faint, ghostly outline of your previous drawing underneath your current canvas.

How to animate:
  1. Draw your first frame (e.g., a person standing).
  2. Click ➕ Add Frame below the canvas.
  3. Click 🧅 Onion: OFF to turn it ON!
  4. You'll see your last drawing faded out. Draw your next frame slightly moved forward.
  5. Keep adding frames to create a walk cycle!
  6. Press ▶️ Play to see it move!
