Skating Game

Glide across the ice in this super colorful 2-player skating adventure! Control your skater with keyboard keys, perform tricks, collect gems, and race with a friend. Built with Three.js for stunning 3D graphics!

Super Smash Fighters

Battle it out in this epic 2-player fighting game! Jump, attack, and use special moves to defeat your opponent. Same keyboard multiplayer action with colorful 3D graphics built with Three.js. First to zero health loses!

Parkour Runner

Run, jump, slide, and wall-run through an endless 3D city! Avoid obstacles, collect coins, and see how far you can go. Speed increases as you progress, making it more challenging. Built with Three.js for smooth parkour action!

3D Dash

Race through a 3D world in this Geometry Dash-inspired game! Jump over spikes, slide under walls, and collect coins with a friend. Two players, same keyboard, endless fun! Speed increases as you go - can you survive?
