# 🌍 How to Make Tinkercad Designs Public

## Quick Steps to Make a Design Public

### For Individual Designs:

1. **Open the Design**
   - Log into Tinkercad
   - Open the design you want to make public

2. **Click the "Share" Button**
   - Look for the **"Share"** button in the top right corner of the Tinkercad editor
   - It's usually next to the "Design" and "Tinker" buttons

3. **Change Privacy Settings**
   - In the Share dialog, you'll see privacy options
   - Click on the privacy dropdown (usually says "Private" or "Only people with the link")
   - Select **"Public"**

4. **Copy the Share Link**
   - Once set to Public, you'll see a share link
   - Copy this link (it looks like: `https://www.tinkercad.com/things/ABC123...`)
   - This is the link you'll use in the portfolio system

## 🏫 For Tinkercad Classroom Accounts

If you're using **Tinkercad Classrooms** (with a class code):

### Option 1: Make Individual Student Designs Public

1. **As the Teacher:**
   - Log into your Tinkercad account
   - Go to your **Dashboard** → **Classes**
   - Click on your class
   - You'll see all student designs

2. **For Each Design:**
   - Click on a student's design to open it
   - Click **"Share"** button
   - Change privacy to **"Public"**
   - Copy the share link

### Option 2: Have Students Make Their Own Designs Public

**Instructions for Students:**

1. Log into Tinkercad with your class code
2. Open your design
3. Click the **"Share"** button (top right)
4. Change from "Private" to **"Public"**
5. Copy the share link and share it with your teacher

### Option 3: Bulk Settings (If Available)

Some Tinkercad Classroom features allow you to:
- Set default privacy settings for new designs
- Check if there's a "Class Settings" option in your classroom dashboard
- Look for privacy/visibility settings in class management

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Privacy Considerations:
- **Public designs** can be viewed by anyone with the link
- They can also appear in Tinkercad's public gallery
- Make sure students understand this before making designs public
- Consider getting parent permission if needed

### For Portfolio Embedding:
- **Only Public designs** can be embedded in the portfolio 3D viewer
- Private designs will show a link but no interactive viewer
- The embed URL format is: `https://www.tinkercad.com/embed/[PROJECT_ID]`

## 🔍 Finding the Share Button

The Share button location:
- **Desktop/Web**: Top right corner, next to "Design" and "Tinker" buttons
- **Mobile**: May be in a menu (three dots or hamburger icon)
- **Icon**: Usually looks like a link/chain icon or says "Share"

## 🎯 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see a Public option"
- Make sure you're logged into the correct account
- Some accounts may have restrictions - check account settings
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure you're the owner of the design

### "The design won't embed"
- Double-check it's set to **Public** (not "Only people with link")
- Verify the URL format is correct
- Try opening the share link in an incognito/private window to test

### "Students can't make designs public"
- Check classroom settings - there may be a "Safe Mode" that restricts sharing
- As teacher, you may need to make designs public yourself
- Check if there are age restrictions on the account

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Create a Template Design**: Make one design public as a template, then students can remix it
2. **Use Collections**: Organize public designs into collections for easy sharing
3. **Share Links Early**: Get share links when designs are still works-in-progress
4. **Test Embedding**: Always test the embed URL in the portfolio admin page before adding to student portfolios
