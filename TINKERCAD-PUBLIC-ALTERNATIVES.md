# 🔧 Tinkercad Public Setting - Alternatives & Solutions

## If You Can't Find "Public" Option

The "Public" setting might not be visible if:
- Your Tinkercad Classroom has "Safe Mode" enabled
- The account type restricts public sharing
- The design is owned by a student account with restrictions

## ✅ Alternative Solutions

### Option 1: Use "Only people with the link" (Works for Embedding!)

**Good news:** You might not need "Public"! Try this:

1. Click **"Share"** button
2. Look for **"Only people with the link"** option
3. Select that option
4. Copy the share link
5. **Test it** - this often works for embedding too!

The embed URL will still work if the design is set to "Only people with the link" - it just won't appear in Tinkercad's public gallery.

### Option 2: Remix/Copy to Your Teacher Account

1. **As the teacher**, log into your Tinkercad account
2. Have students share their design link with you (even if private)
3. Open the design link
4. Click **"Tinker this"** or **"Remix"** button
5. This creates a copy in YOUR account
6. Now YOU can make YOUR copy public
7. Use your copy's share link for the portfolio

### Option 3: Export and Re-upload (If Needed)

1. Student exports their design as STL or OBJ
2. You upload it to your Tinkercad account
3. Make your version public
4. Use that link

### Option 4: Check Classroom Settings

1. Go to your **Tinkercad Classroom dashboard**
2. Look for **"Class Settings"** or **"Privacy Settings"**
3. Check for **"Safe Mode"** or **"Sharing Restrictions"**
4. Disable safe mode if you want students to share publicly
5. Save settings

### Option 5: Use the Share Link Anyway

**Try this first:** Even if you can't set it to "Public", try using the share link in the portfolio system. Sometimes the embed works with:
- "Only people with the link" 
- "Class only" visibility
- Even some private settings

## 🎯 Step-by-Step: What to Look For

When you click "Share", you should see options like:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ Share Design                │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│ Privacy: [Dropdown ▼]       │
│                             │
│ Options might include:      │
│ • Private                   │
│ • Only people with link      │
│ • Class only                │
│ • Public                    │ ← Look for this
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

**If you don't see "Public":**
- Try "Only people with the link" first
- Check if there's a settings icon next to the dropdown
- Look for "Advanced" or "More options"

## 🔍 Where Exactly is the Share Button?

### Desktop/Web:
- **Top right corner** of the Tinkercad editor
- Usually has a **link/chain icon** or says **"Share"**
- Next to "Design", "Tinker", and "Export" buttons

### If You Still Can't Find It:
1. Look for a **three-dot menu** (⋮) in the top right
2. Check the **design name** at the top - might be clickable
3. Try right-clicking on the design thumbnail in your dashboard
4. Look in the **design properties** or **settings** panel

## 💡 Pro Tip: Test the Embed

Even if you can't make it "Public", test the embed:

1. Get the share link (any privacy level)
2. Convert to embed URL: `https://www.tinkercad.com/embed/[PROJECT_ID]`
3. Test it in the portfolio admin page
4. If it works, you're good to go!

## 🆘 Still Having Issues?

If none of these work:

1. **Contact Tinkercad Support** - They can help with classroom settings
2. **Use Screenshots** - As a backup, you can use screenshots of the 3D models
3. **Export as Images** - Tinkercad can export nice images of designs
4. **Use the Link Only** - Portfolio can show links even without embed
