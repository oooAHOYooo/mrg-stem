# Aether Academy: Brand Identity & Philosophy

## The Name
**Aether Academy**
*   **Definition:** "Aether" represents the upper air, the fifth element, and the medium through which light and digital signals travel.
*   **Philosophy:** We are not just teaching STEM; we are teaching students how to architect reality within the digital ether.

## Visual Identity
*   **Primary Palette:** Obsidian Black (`#050505`), Pure White (`#ffffff`), and Ethereal Cyan/Aqua (`#00f2ff`).
*   **Typography:** 'Outfit' or 'Inter'—clean, geometric, and modern.
*   **Vibe:** Minimalist, high-contrast, premium. Think "Apple Store in Space."

## Key Terminology (The "Elite" Vocabulary)
- **The Nexus:** The central hub (Physical school in New Haven).
*   **Kinetic Deployment:** The mobile lab (Taking it on the road).
*   **Architects:** What we call our students (not "kids" or "pupils").
*   **The Protocol:** Our unique curriculum.
