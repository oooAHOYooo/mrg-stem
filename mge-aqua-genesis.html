🥊 Make a Smash Bros Game!

Let's build a fighting arena!

  1. Click Green (World) 🌍 to make a floor stage.
  2. Click Blue (Cube) 🟦 to add Player 1 (uses WASD keys).
  3. Click Red (Sphere) 🔴 to add Player 2 (uses Arrow keys).
  4. Click Pink (Jump) 🦘 to make the stage or players bounce.
  5. If you click Random Cube 🎲, a randomly colored player drops in!

Try to push each other off the edge to win! If you fall off, you will respawn back in the center.
