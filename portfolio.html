📋 How to Prepare Your Work for Mr. G

  1. Click the big blue button that says "➕ Add a Project to Submit"
  2. Fill out the form:
    • Give your project a name (like "My Awesome Game")
    • Choose what type it is (Tinkercad, Scratch, Video, or Other)
    • Write about what you made and what you learned
    • Add links to your work (like your Scratch project URL)
    • Add tags (like "game" or "coding")
  3. Click "Add to Collection" - Your project is now ready to submit!
  4. When you're ready, click "📮 Submit All to Mr. G" to send all your work to Mr. Gonzalez to immortalize it!

💡 Need Help?

❓ How do I add a project to submit?

Click the big blue "➕ Add a Project to Submit" button at the top of the page. Fill out the form with information about your project!

✏️ Can I edit my projects before submitting?

Yes! Click the ✏️ Edit button on any project to change the information. Make sure everything is perfect before you submit to Mr. G!

🗑️ How do I remove a project from my collection?

Click the 🗑️ Delete button on any project. It will ask you to confirm before removing it from your collection.

📮 When should I submit to Mr. G?

Submit whenever you have projects you're proud of! You can submit multiple times throughout the year. Mr. G will review and immortalize all your amazing work!

⭐ What happens after I submit?

Mr. Gonzalez will review your work and make sure it's saved forever! Your projects will be celebrated and preserved for everyone to see!
