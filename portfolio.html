📋 How to Add a Project

  1. Click the big blue button that says "➕ Add a New Project"
  2. Fill out the form:
    • Give your project a name (like "My Awesome Game")
    • Choose what type it is (Tinkercad, Scratch, Video, or Other)
    • Write about what you made and what you learned
    • Add links to your work (like your Scratch project URL)
    • Add tags (like "game" or "coding")
  3. Click "Add to Portfolio" - Your project is now saved!
  4. See your project appear in your portfolio below!

💡 Need Help?

❓ How do I add a project?

Click the big blue "➕ Add a New Project" button at the top of the page. Fill out the form with information about your project!

✏️ Can I edit my projects?

Yes! Click the ✏️ Edit button on any project to change the information. You can update it anytime!

🗑️ How do I delete a project?

Click the 🗑️ Delete button on any project. It will ask you to confirm before deleting.

💾 What does "Save My Portfolio" do?

This downloads a backup of your portfolio to your computer. You can save it and import it later if needed!

🔗 How do I share my portfolio?

Once your teacher sets up the backend, you'll get a special link to share your portfolio with others!
