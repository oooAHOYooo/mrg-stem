Class Expectations

Building Your Path to Success | Grades 1-5

1

Grade 1 Expectations

  • Walk in slowly and find your station
  • Choose your tools—computer, blocks, or digital—and start working
  • Do your own work and stay focused on your project
  • Clean up your station when you're done
  • Treat everything with respect—the bricks, the computers, and all tools
  • Raise your hand if you need help and wait patiently for Mr. Gonzalez

🎯 Your Choice Matters

You get to decide: work on the computer with Scratch or Tinkercad, build with blocks, or explore digital tools. Choose what you want to learn.

2

Grade 2 Expectations

  • Walk in slowly and find your station
  • Choose your tools—computer, blocks, or digital—and start working
  • Do your own work and stay focused on your project
  • Clean up your station when you're done
  • Treat everything with respect—the bricks, the computers, and all tools
  • Raise your hand if you need help and wait patiently for Mr. Gonzalez

🎯 Your Choice Matters

You choose: work on computer programming, build structures with blocks, or explore digital design. Pick what you want to do.

3

Grade 3 Expectations

  • Walk in slowly, find your station, and get started right away
  • Choose your tools—computer, blocks, or digital—and work on your project
  • Do your own work and stay focused until class ends
  • Clean up your station and put everything away when finished
  • Treat everything with respect—the bricks, the computers, and all tools
  • Raise your hand if you need help and wait patiently for Mr. Gonzalez

🎯 Your Choice Matters

You choose: computer programming and game design, engineering with blocks, or digital art and design. The choice is yours.

4

Grade 4 Expectations

  • Walk in slowly, find your station, and begin working immediately
  • Choose your tools—computer, blocks, or digital—and stick with your choice
  • Do your own work, stay focused, and complete your projects
  • Clean up your station completely and help others when asked
  • Treat everything with respect—the bricks, the computers, and all tools
  • Raise your hand if you need help and wait patiently for Mr. Gonzalez

🎯 Your Choice Matters

You decide: computer programming and game development, structures with blocks, or digital art. The choice is yours.

5

Grade 5 Expectations

  • Walk in slowly, find your station, and start working right away
  • Choose your tools—computer, blocks, or digital—and work independently
  • Do your own work, stay focused the entire class, and finish your projects
  • Clean up your station, help others, and be ready to share your work
  • Treat everything with respect—the bricks, the computers, and all tools
  • Raise your hand if you need help and wait patiently for Mr. Gonzalez

🎯 Your Choice Matters

You choose: computer programming and game development, engineering with blocks, or digital experiences. The choice is yours.

What I Expect from Everyone

I want you to do your own work. Find something you love and keep working on it all year long.

You decide: Do you want to work on the computer? Or with blocks? Or with digital tools? You choose! I will help you learn whatever you pick.
