Smart Investing for Kids!

🏢 Single Stocks: High risk! Like betting on one player in a game. If they win, you win big! If they lose, you lose big.

📦 Funds (ETFs): Healthy! Like buying the whole team. If one player is tired, the others can still help you win. This is called Diversification.

🛡️ Bonds: Very Safe! Like a savings piggy bank that slowly but surely adds money every day. Good for when you want to protect your profit.
