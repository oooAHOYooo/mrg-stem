What is a Stock?

A Stock is like a tiny piece of a company. When you buy a stock in Apple or Tesla, you own a tiny slice of that company!

If the company does well, your "slice" becomes worth more money. If they have problems, it might become worth less.

Goal: Buy slices when they are cheap (Green) and sell them when they are expensive (Red)!
