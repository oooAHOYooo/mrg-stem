🧱 Rokenbok Blocks – What You Can Build

Get inspired! See all the amazing things kids build with Rokenbok—tracks, ramps, elevators, vehicles, cities, and more.

🌟 Kids Everywhere Are Building with Rokenbok!

Rokenbok is a building system where blocks snap together to make tracks, structures, vehicles, and whole worlds. See what’s possible and start planning your next build!

🛤️ Tracks & Roller Coasters

Build tracks for balls and vehicles! Create loops, drops, curves, and bridges. Design your own roller coasters and see how far you can make things roll.

🔄 Loops & Curves

Snap together curves and loops so balls or vehicles can go around and around.

📐 Ramps & Drops

Add ramps and drops to speed things up and make exciting runs.

🌉 Bridges

Build bridges that carry tracks over other parts of your layout.

⬆️ Elevators & Lifts

Move things up and down! Build elevators and lifts to bring balls or cargo to different levels. Great for learning about simple machines and cause-and-effect.

💡 Ideas:

  • Lift balls to the top of a track so they roll down
  • Connect multiple levels with one elevator
  • Use elevators in a factory or city layout

🚗 Vehicles & Moving Parts

Rokenbok sets often include vehicles and moving parts. Combine them with your tracks and structures for action-packed builds.

🚂 Cars & Trains

Run vehicles on your tracks and design stations or parking.

🏗️ Cranes & Loaders

Add cranes and loaders to move “cargo” and run a mini factory.

⚙️ Gears & Motors

Use gears and motors to make things spin, lift, or move automatically.

🏙️ Cities & Structures

Go big! Build cities, towers, factories, and landscapes. Combine tracks, elevators, and vehicles into one huge Rokenbok world.

🎯 Build Ideas:

  • City: Buildings, roads, and a train or ball run through the middle
  • Factory: Conveyors, elevators, and loading zones
  • Theme Park: Roller coasters, ramps, and loops
  • Space Station: Multi-level layout with lifts and tracks

🔬 STEM Skills You Practice

When you build with Rokenbok, you’re doing real engineering and problem-solving!

📐 Engineering

Plan structures that stay up and carry weight. Test and improve your designs.

⚡ Physics

See how slope, speed, and gravity affect balls and vehicles on your tracks.

🧩 Problem-Solving

Fix jams, redesign curves, and make your layout run smoothly from start to finish.

🎨 Creativity

There’s no single “right” build—you decide what to make and how it works.
