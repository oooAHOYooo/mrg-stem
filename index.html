🎮 How to Make Collectible Items in Scratch

Learn how to add coins, stars, or other collectible items to your Scratch game. This is one of the most important “game dev” skills: it teaches you collision detection, scoring, and how to make your game feel rewarding.

💡 What you’ll learn: collision detection, variables for score, “spawn” logic, and how to make collectibles disappear when you touch them.
