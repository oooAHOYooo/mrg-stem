✨ What is a Portfolio?

Your portfolio is your personal showcase of creativity and learning. It's a place where you can:

  • Show off your best projects
  • Track your progress and growth
  • Add new work as you create it
  • Improve and update existing projects
  • Share your achievements with others

📋 Today's Lesson Plan

  1. Choose Your Project: Select one of the project options below that interests you most
  2. Start Creating: Begin working on your chosen project using the tools and resources provided
  3. Document Your Work: Take screenshots, save your files, and write notes about what you created
  4. Add to Portfolio: Add your completed project to your portfolio with a description
  5. Share & Improve: Share your work with classmates and continue to improve your portfolio items

📅 Tomorrow's Plan

  1. Warm-up (5 min): Open your project and show today’s progress to a partner
  2. Build (20–30 min): Add 1 new feature (obstacle, enemy, powerup, or level)
  3. Test (10 min): Fix 2 bugs (ask a friend to find them!)
  4. Portfolio (5 min): Submit your best link + 1 sentence reflection
