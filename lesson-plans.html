January

Week 2

  • Mini challenge: add checkpoints

Week 3

  • Mini challenge: player vs player tag

Week 4

  • Lesson 4: Projectiles + Powerups

  • Test & debug day

Week 5

  • Final Challenge (build your own game)
  • Peer playtesting day
  • Submit to teacher for showcase
